Answers to the specific questions in the brief:

- Which features matter: in every study with an ablation or ranking, the most recent realised imbalance signal dominates (past SI and NRV in Van Gompel; last known price and the 15-min-old activated bid prices in Martens; historical NIV in the UK study; imbalance volume in Lucas). Day-ahead price is second in Martens. The MRINN ablation puts the balancing-energy block (aFRR/mFRR activations) and the scarcity block (SI) ahead of the market-reference block (DA + ID). Weather enters only through PV/wind forecasts in the Elia work; the UU weather-only day-ahead study reports no numbers.
- Which horizons are predictable: Browell and Gilbert show skill decaying from ≈40 % MAE gain at 0 h to ≈5 % at 5 h, with day-ahead skill of 3 %. Dumas shows NMAE rising from 52,7 % (15 min) to 72,6 % (360 min). Elia and the Ghent group only forecast the current and next one or two QH. Nordic review cited by Martens: day-ahead imbalance forecasts "not possible".
- Did intraday prices help, and by how much: Narajewski (DE, 30 min ahead) finds the ID1 index itself is the best point forecast and nothing beats it on CRPS/MAE/RMSE; sophisticated models only fix coverage. MRINN (AT) finds the ID15/ID60 naive equal to persistence (AQL 25,49 vs 25,46) and removing the whole DA+ID block costs about 10 % AQL (20,70 to 22,73). Van Gompel: intraday unmatched volume does not help SI. No paper tested intraday auction prices (IDA1/2/3) as features; not found. No paper used Dutch intraday data because it is not open (Martens).

## 3. TSO publications

Elia (BE)
- System-imbalance forecast: current QH (ods136) and next QH (ods147), refreshed every minute, point forecast plus probability bins; on Open Data "since October 2022" (https://innovation.eliagroup.eu/projects/simplify-advanced-machine-learning-to-support-balancing-the-system). Accuracy: "the RMSE of the SI forecasts for the next quarter hour have been reduced from an initial 170 MW to around 100 MW" (same page). Elia states the forecasts "can have a significant error margin" and are not binding (ods147 description).
- Imbalance price forecast (IPF): snippet of https://www.elia.be/en/grid-data/balancing/imbalance-prices-forecasts (page 403): proof of concept September to November 2024, then permanent publication; forecast of the imbalance price for the upcoming QH, published one minute before the QH starts, with a confidence interval and the drivers (aFRR, mFRR, cap, floor, deadband); ML models retrained continuously. The 2024-09-13 info-session slides (snippet) say it is "available on request", takes only the main price component into account, and carries a High/Medium/Low confidence indicator. No accuracy figures found. The Open Data catalogue holds no price-forecast dataset; the per-minute price (ods161) is a running estimate of the current ISP, not a forecast.
- Elia also lists ods211 "[Simulated] Imbalance prices per minute (Live updates as of 24/11/2026 ... conditional on CREG approval)" (catalogue API, https://opendata.elia.be/api/explore/v2.1/catalog/datasets?where=%22imbalance%22&limit=50).

TenneT (NL)
- No forecast of the imbalance price or of the next ISP's regulation state found. TenneT publishes the balance delta every 12 s with a 2-minute delay since 25 November 2025, with IGCC, PICASSO, aFRR and incident-reserve volumes and the highest and lowest active aFRR bid prices (https://solarmagazine.nl/nieuws-zonne-energie/i42301/tennet-publiceert-balansdelta-voortaan-om-de-12-seconden). Regulation state is derived from the 12-s delta since 3 February 2026; the price itself stays on 4-s activations (same article). The TenneT page (snippet, 403: https://www.tennet.eu/nl-en/markets/transparency/current-network-data/balance-delta) says the minute snapshots "provide an indication of the final settlement price" and the 1-minute delta "will be phased out on May 6th 2026".
- Delay history: 3 to 5 minutes at the end of 2024 (https://solarmagazine.nl/nieuws-zonne-energie/i39044/tennet-grijpt-in-vanwege-regeltoestand-2-partijen-varen-nu-lange-tijd-in-de-mist), back to 2 minutes in July 2025 because the measure "blijkt zelfs onvoldoende effectief" (https://solarmagazine.nl/nieuws-zonne-energie/i40864/tennet-verkort-balansdelta-publicatievertraging-naar-2-minuten).
- Regulation-state-2 share per TenneT: about 8 % 2016 to 2023, 16,8 % in 2024, 27,3 % in 2025, around 40 % in 2026 with monthly peaks to 50 %; PICASSO since October 2024; extremes near −4 000 and +4 400 EUR/MWh; "voor batterijen geldt dat daar minder mee kan worden verdiend op de onbalansmarkt" (https://solarmagazine.nl/nieuws-zonne-energie/i43941/het-dilemma-picasso-minder-negatieve-onbalansprijzen-goed-voor-de-onbalansmarkt).
- API products: settlement prices, settled imbalance volumes, balance delta high res, merit order lists (aFRR/mFRRsa, incident reserve), FRR activations; key via registration (https://developer.acc.tennet.eu).

Germany: reBAP "published once a month" (Narajewski p10), no nowcast; "Regulation may also explicitly forbid speculation based on imbalance prices, as in Germany" (Browell and Gilbert p6).

Great Britain: no TSO price forecast found; Elexon publishes indicative data, Modo Energy sells a live NIV/system-price estimate "within 1 % of Elexon's official values in 92 % of settlement periods for system price, and 73 % for NIV" (https://developers.modoenergy.com/docs/modo-realtime-system-imbalance-calculations).

## 5. Verdict

There is no pre-made algorithm to adopt. Nothing open-source forecasts the TenneT imbalance price or trades a home battery on it; the Dutch open-source landscape stops at data clients (TenneTeu-py, tennet_balance) and result ledgers (frank-energie-slim, onbalansmarkt.com). The only forecaster with code is MRINN, built on Austrian rules, and its value is the design, not the weights. The literature agrees on the shape of the problem: skill lives inside the next one or two quarter-hours and collapses toward climatology by 3 to 5 hours (Browell and Gilbert, Dumas); the most recent realised imbalance signal is the dominant feature everywhere (Van Gompel, Martens, the UK NIV study), with day-ahead price second and aFRR/mFRR activation prices ahead of market-reference prices in the one ablation that separates them (MRINN); intraday prices are not a free lunch, since the ID1 index was unbeatable 30 min ahead in Germany and the ID-index naive equalled persistence in Austria, and nobody has tested IDA1/2/3 auction prices; and the strongest published designs are two-stage, system imbalance first and then a rule-based map to price (Yu and Bunn review, Dumas, Pavirani, MRINN). The starting point for the planned 15-minute decision loop is therefore: a TenneT-specific two-stage model that nowcasts the balance delta and the regulation-state-2 probability for the current and next ISP from the 12-s delta with its 2-minute delay, the highest and lowest activated bid prices, IGCC/PICASSO volumes and the running minute price, then maps to a price distribution through the published merit order with day-ahead, lagged settled prices and calendar as the secondary block, and IDA prices as a feature to A/B rather than a given. Model family: quantile gradient boosting (LightGBM/XGBoost) or a small MLP with a hierarchical quantile head, since the deep backbones in MRINN gained nothing over MLP/XGB beyond the rule block. Evaluate with CRPS and rMAE against two naives (persistence of the running minute price, and the day-ahead price) and with Martens' wrong-side-of-day-ahead rate, because direction is what the battery pays for. For the earnings ledger, the comparable benchmark is onbalansmarkt.com's EUR/kW per month (about 10 to 19 EUR/kW in September 2026), with the RVO and VEH reports as the record that imbalance income has fallen since 2024 and regulation state 2 now covers around 40 % of quarter-hours.
