## Recommendation

1. IDA results: adopt the Nord Pool data-portal API (`/api/DayAheadPrices?market=SIDC_IntradayAuction{1,2,3}&deliveryArea=NL`). It is JSON, 15-min, tokenless, carries all three auctions back to 2024-06-14, publishes about 17 min after each gate (15:17, 22:17 CET on D-1; 10:16 CET on D), and on 29 Oct its values equal the EPEX page to the cent. Backfill: one `DayAheadPrices` call per day and auction (about 2 600 calls at 1/s), `AggregatePrices` for a quick daily-stat sanity check. Keep the EPEX page as a cross-check at most three loads per day: its WAF challenge trips after roughly a dozen requests, and it serves stale parameter sets when hit in quick succession. Note the Nord Pool website terms (no automatic extraction, no redistribution) before publishing anything derived from it.
2. Continuous: reachable without paying, with limits. The Nord Pool `IntradayMarketStatistics` route gives near-live (15-min refresh) per-quarter-hour VWAP, last-1h VWAP, high/low/close and volume, but only a rolling D-7..D+1 window, so a recorder must start now to build history. The EPEX Continuous page gives the official ID1/ID3/IDFull per contract, end of day, under the same WAF limit and an internal-use-only banner. Trade ticks and history beyond 7 days are paid only: cheapest list prices are Nord Pool "Trades only" from EUR 4 700/yr or EPEX continuous Histo EUR 6 050/yr; Montel, EnAppSys and Energy Quantified do not publish prices.
3. TenneT live: register a free developer key and poll `balance-delta-high-res/latest` five times per minute (2-min delay, 12-s points, 30-min window); the HACS integration `aritmeester/tennet_balance` already does this. Keep the ORDS route for settled ISP prices and use the Turnstile-gated download page for one-off history.
